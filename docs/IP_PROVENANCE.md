## Ownership practice

- Product source, original interface copy, and original visual design are kept in this repository.
- Work is committed in meaningful increments so authorship and development dates remain reviewable.
- Third-party packages must remain listed in the package manifest and lockfile with their own licences intact.
- Lidl names may be used only to identify compatibility. Lidl logos and official promotional artwork must not be copied into this repository without permission.
- Customer QR images, receipts, credentials, secrets, and production data must never be committed.

## Product boundary

CouponShare is an independent group coordination product. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Lidl. QR owners must choose what to upload and which closed group members may view it. Sharing can be withdrawn, although the product cannot prevent a recipient from taking a screenshot outside the application.
